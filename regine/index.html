ReGINE is an alternative Logue-compatible runtime that is compatible with logue v1l; Prologue/NTS-1/Minilogue, logue v2; NTS-1 mkII and NTS-3, and drumlogue synth and microKORG2 oscillator and effect units. What was mostly created to provide an alternative test platform, is now a functional implementation that even allows logue v1, and NTS-2 mkII to run on the drumlogue and microKORG2 or even convert NTS-1 to NTS1 mkII.
